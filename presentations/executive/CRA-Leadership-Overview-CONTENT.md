## Slide 2 — The Problem We Are Solving

**Heading:** Every CRA engagement starts from scratch — and it costs us

**Three bullets (large font, short):**
- Delivery teams spend 3–6 weeks scoping and building templates before an assessment even starts
- Inconsistent outputs across engagements create QA risk and weaken partner trust
- Customers can get a free 15-minute Azure check from Microsoft — we must compete on depth, not speed

**Bottom callout box (Rackspace Red):**  
> "Without a standardised framework, we win deals on relationships and lose renewals on quality."

**Speaker notes:**  
The status quo is that every CRA engagement is custom-built. An architect spends weeks recreating templates, scoping documents, and scoring models that already exist somewhere on someone's laptop. This creates three problems: it slows time-to-value for customers, it makes our outputs inconsistent (which matters when Microsoft and AWS are auditing our deliverables for AMM/MAP funding), and it means junior architects can't ramp up quickly. The CRA framework solves all three.

## Appendix B — Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is this only for Azure engagements?**  
No. The framework is cloud-neutral and covers AWS, Azure, and GCP equally. Azure is the primary recommendation for DMG Media UK, but the framework supports any hyperscaler outcome.

**Q: Has this been used in a real engagement?**  
Yes. The framework was used in the DMG Media UK engagement (February–June 2026) — 4,212 VMs, 3-way hyperscaler evaluation, Azure recommendation with AMM funding identified.

**Q: What is the legal status of the DMG case study?**  
Both the working repo and the final rxt-mpc/ps-ind-cloud-readiness-accelerator repo will be private. The case study is cleared for internal use. External sharing requires legal review (in progress).

**Q: Who owns the framework long-term?**  
Rackspace Cloud Solutions Architecture team. Maintenance target: 0.1 FTE ongoing after initial build-out is complete.

**Q: How does this relate to existing Rackspace frameworks?**  
CRA is specifically for the assessment phase (pre-migration). It is complementary to Rackspace's managed services and migration delivery frameworks, which handle execution. CRA produces the deliverable that leads to Part 2.
