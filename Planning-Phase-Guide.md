# Cloud Readiness Accelerator - Planning Phase Guide

## Phase Overview

The Planning Phase translates evaluation results into detailed, actionable plans for cloud migration execution. This phase focuses on developing comprehensive migration roadmaps, planning resources and timelines, establishing governance structures, and aligning stakeholders on migration strategy and execution approach. The primary objectives are to create detailed migration plans, establish governance and organizational structures, plan resource requirements, and prepare the organization for migration execution.

### Phase Objectives

1. Develop detailed migration roadmap with waves, timelines, and milestones
2. Plan resource requirements and organizational structure
3. Establish governance and decision-making frameworks
4. Develop detailed risk mitigation and contingency plans
5. Plan stakeholder engagement and change management
6. Develop detailed technical migration plans
7. Establish success criteria and validation procedures
8. Prepare organization for migration execution

### Phase Duration and Resources

- **Typical Duration**: 2-4 weeks (varies by organization size)
- **Small Organizations (10-50 apps)**: 1-2 weeks
- **Mid-Market Organizations (50-200 apps)**: 2-3 weeks
- **Enterprise Organizations (200+ apps)**: 3-4 weeks

### Resource Requirements

- **Program Manager**: 1 FTE
- **Cloud Architects**: 2-3 FTE
- **Technical Leads**: 2-3 FTE
- **Change Management Lead**: 1 FTE
- **Governance Lead**: 1 FTE
- **Financial Manager**: 0.5 FTE

## Phase Entry and Exit Criteria

### Entry Criteria

Before starting the Planning Phase, ensure:

1. **Evaluation Complete**: Evaluation Phase completed with hyperscaler recommendations and business case
2. **Stakeholder Sign-off**: Executive sponsor and steering committee approved evaluation results
3. **Migration Strategy Approved**: Cloud platform selection and migration approach approved
4. **Planning Team Ready**: Planning team assembled and trained
5. **Planning Tools Prepared**: Planning templates and tools prepared

### Exit Criteria

The Planning Phase is complete when:

1. **Detailed Roadmap**: Detailed migration roadmap developed with waves, timelines, and milestones
2. **Resource Plan**: Resource requirements and organizational structure planned
3. **Governance Established**: Governance structure and decision-making frameworks established
4. **Risk Plans**: Detailed risk mitigation and contingency plans developed
5. **Change Management**: Change management and stakeholder engagement plans developed
6. **Technical Plans**: Detailed technical migration plans developed
7. **Success Criteria**: Success criteria and validation procedures defined
8. **Stakeholder Alignment**: Stakeholders aligned on migration strategy and execution approach

## Step-by-Step Activity Instructions

### Activity 1: Detailed Roadmap Development

**Objective**: Develop detailed migration roadmap with waves, timelines, and milestones

**Duration**: 4-6 days

**Steps**:

1. **Refine Wave Planning**
   - Review wave groupings from Evaluation Phase
   - Refine wave groupings based on detailed dependency analysis
   - Identify applications that must migrate together
   - Identify applications suitable for parallel migration
   - Document wave refinements and rationale

2. **Develop Wave Timelines**
   - Estimate duration for each wave
   - Identify critical path and dependencies
   - Plan for parallel execution where possible
   - Identify key milestones for each wave
   - Document timeline assumptions and constraints

3. **Plan Wave Sequencing**
   - Sequence waves to minimize operational risk
   - Sequence waves to manage resource constraints
   - Sequence waves to align with business priorities
   - Identify wave dependencies and constraints
   - Document sequencing rationale

4. **Develop Detailed Milestones**
   - Identify key milestones for each wave
   - Identify key milestones for overall program
   - Define milestone completion criteria
   - Identify milestone dependencies
   - Document milestone schedule

5. **Develop Contingency Plans**
   - Identify contingency scenarios (delays, failures, resource constraints)
   - Develop contingency timelines
   - Identify contingency resource requirements
   - Document contingency triggers and activation procedures
   - Prepare contingency communication plans

6. **Prepare Roadmap Documentation**
   - Create detailed migration roadmap (Gantt chart or timeline)
   - Document wave descriptions and objectives
   - Document wave timelines and milestones
   - Document resource requirements by wave
   - Document roadmap assumptions and constraints

**Deliverables**:
- Detailed Migration Roadmap (Gantt chart or timeline)
- Wave Descriptions and Objectives
- Wave Timelines and Milestones
- Contingency Plans and Timelines
- Roadmap Assumptions and Constraints Documentation

**Best Practices**:
- Involve operations and technical teams in roadmap development
- Plan for realistic timelines with contingency buffers
- Identify critical path and dependencies
- Plan for parallel execution where possible
- Document assumptions and constraints clearly

**Common Pitfalls**:
- Overly optimistic timelines without contingency buffers
- Inadequate consideration of dependencies
- Insufficient resource planning
- Not planning for parallel execution
- Inadequate contingency planning

### Activity 2: Resource Planning and Organizational Structure

**Objective**: Plan resource requirements and establish organizational structure

**Duration**: 3-5 days

**Steps**:

1. **Define Organizational Structure**

2. **Identify Resource Requirements**
   - Identify resource requirements by role and wave

   - Identify resource gaps and training needs
   - Document resource requirements by phase

3. **Plan Resource Allocation**
   - Allocate resources to waves and activities
   - Plan for resource ramp-up and ramp-down
   - Identify resource conflicts and constraints
   - Plan for resource cross-training
   - Document resource allocation plan

4. **Plan Staffing and Hiring**
   - Identify staffing gaps and hiring needs
   - Plan recruitment and onboarding timeline
   - Identify training and certification needs
   - Plan training and development activities
   - Document staffing plan

5. **Plan Vendor and Partner Engagement**
   - Identify vendor and partner requirements
   - Plan vendor selection and contracting
   - Plan vendor management and oversight
   - Identify vendor dependencies and constraints
   - Document vendor engagement plan

6. **Prepare Resource Plan Documentation**
   - Create resource plan with requirements by role and wave
   - Document organizational structure and roles
   - Document resource allocation and scheduling
   - Document staffing and hiring plan
   - Document vendor engagement plan

**Deliverables**:
- Organizational Structure and Roles Documentation
- Resource Requirements Plan (by role, skill, and wave)
- Resource Allocation and Scheduling Plan
- Staffing and Hiring Plan
- Vendor and Partner Engagement Plan
- Resource Plan Summary

**Best Practices**:
- Involve HR and finance in resource planning
- Plan for realistic resource availability
- Identify resource gaps and training needs early
- Plan for resource ramp-up and ramp-down
- Document resource constraints and dependencies

**Common Pitfalls**:
- Underestimating resource requirements
- Inadequate consideration of resource availability
- Not planning for training and development
- Insufficient vendor management planning
- Inadequate documentation of resource constraints

### Activity 4: Risk Mitigation and Contingency Planning

**Objective**: Develop detailed risk mitigation and contingency plans

**Duration**: 3-5 days

**Steps**:

1. **Review and Refine Risk Assessment**
   - Review risks identified in Evaluation Phase
   - Identify additional risks specific to planning phase
   - Assess risk likelihood and impact
   - Prioritize risks by score
   - Document risk assessment update

2. **Develop Risk Mitigation Strategies**
   - Develop mitigation strategy for each high-priority risk
   - Identify mitigation actions and owners
   - Estimate mitigation cost and timeline
   - Assess residual risk after mitigation
   - Document mitigation plan

3. **Develop Contingency Plans**
   - Identify contingency scenarios (delays, failures, resource constraints)
   - Develop contingency response procedures
   - Identify contingency triggers and activation procedures
   - Estimate contingency cost and timeline
   - Document contingency plans

4. **Plan Disaster Recovery and Rollback**
   - Develop rollback procedures for each wave
   - Identify rollback triggers and decision criteria
   - Estimate rollback timeline and cost
   - Plan for data recovery and restoration
   - Document disaster recovery and rollback procedures

5. **Establish Risk Monitoring and Reporting**
   - Define risk monitoring procedures
   - Define risk reporting procedures and cadence
   - Define risk escalation procedures
   - Define risk review and update procedures
   - Document risk monitoring and reporting procedures

6. **Prepare Risk and Contingency Documentation**
   - Create risk register with mitigation strategies
   - Document contingency plans and procedures
   - Document disaster recovery and rollback procedures
   - Document risk monitoring and reporting procedures
   - Create risk and contingency summary

**Deliverables**:
- Updated Risk Assessment and Register
- Risk Mitigation Plans (by risk)
- Contingency Plans and Procedures
- Disaster Recovery and Rollback Procedures
- Risk Monitoring and Reporting Procedures
- Risk and Contingency Summary

**Best Practices**:
- Involve security, compliance, and operations teams in risk planning
- Develop mitigation strategies for high-priority risks
- Plan for contingencies and rollback procedures
- Establish regular risk monitoring and reporting
- Document risk assumptions and dependencies

**Common Pitfalls**:
- Inadequate risk mitigation planning
- Insufficient contingency planning
- Not planning for rollback procedures
- Inadequate risk monitoring and reporting
- Failure to update risk assessment

## Common Challenges and Solutions

### Challenge 1: Unrealistic Timelines

**Problem**: Timelines too aggressive without contingency buffers

**Solutions**:
- Develop realistic timelines based on historical data
- Plan for contingency buffers (10-20% of timeline)
- Identify critical path and dependencies
- Plan for parallel execution where possible
- Review timelines with experienced teams

### Challenge 2: Resource Constraints

**Problem**: Insufficient resources available for migration

**Solutions**:
- Assess resource availability early
- Identify resource gaps and hiring needs
- Plan for vendor and partner engagement
- Plan for cross-training and knowledge transfer
- Prioritize resource allocation to critical activities

### Challenge 3: Governance Complexity

**Problem**: Governance structure too complex or unclear

**Solutions**:
- Establish clear, simple governance structure
- Define clear roles and responsibilities
- Establish efficient decision-making procedures
- Plan for regular governance meetings
- Document governance procedures clearly

### Challenge 4: Risk Underestimation

**Problem**: Risks underestimated or not adequately mitigated

**Solutions**:
- Conduct comprehensive risk assessment
- Involve security, compliance, and operations teams
- Develop mitigation strategies for high-priority risks
- Plan for contingencies and rollback procedures
- Establish regular risk monitoring and reporting

### Challenge 5: Stakeholder Resistance

**Problem**: Stakeholders resistant to migration plan

**Solutions**:
- Assess organizational change readiness
- Develop comprehensive communication plan
- Plan for training and enablement
- Engage stakeholders throughout planning
- Address change resistance proactively

## Conclusion

The Planning Phase translates evaluation results into detailed, actionable plans for cloud migration execution. By developing comprehensive migration roadmaps, planning resources and organizational structures, establishing governance frameworks, and planning for change management, the planning team prepares the organization for successful migration execution.

Success in the Planning Phase requires strong program management, comprehensive planning, clear governance, and effective stakeholder engagement. By following the step-by-step instructions, best practices, and addressing common challenges, planning teams can develop effective plans and prepare the organization for migration execution.

The deliverables from the Planning Phase—detailed roadmap, resource plan, governance structure, risk mitigation plans, and change management plans—form the foundation for successful migration execution and organizational transformation.
