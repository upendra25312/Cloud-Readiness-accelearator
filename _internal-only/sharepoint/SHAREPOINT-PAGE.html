🚀 Getting Started

For New Cloud Migration Projects

Step 1: Initiate Assessment (Week 1)

Use Discovery Phase Guide to identify stakeholders and scope. Complete Application Discovery Template. Map infrastructure and dependencies.

Step 2: Evaluate Readiness (Week 2-3)

Execute Cloud Readiness Scoring using five-dimensional model. Conduct Multi-Cloud Evaluation. Develop Business Case with TCO/ROI analysis.

Step 3: Plan Migration (Week 4)

Assess risks and compliance requirements. Map dependencies and plan migration waves. Create executive summary and migration roadmap.

Step 4: Stakeholder Alignment (Week 5)

Present findings to governance structure. Obtain stakeholder approval. Finalize migration strategy.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Cloud Readiness Accelerator?

The Cloud Readiness Accelerator is a comprehensive framework for conducting systematic cloud readiness assessments. It provides methodology, templates, guides, and integration patterns to help organizations evaluate their cloud migration readiness across multiple dimensions.

Who should use this framework?

Cloud architects, project managers, pre-sales consultants, security officers, and finance teams can use this framework. It's designed for consulting firms, enterprises, and service providers conducting cloud readiness assessments.

How long does an assessment take?

A typical assessment takes 4-5 weeks depending on organization size and complexity. Small organizations (10-50 apps) may complete in 2-3 weeks, while enterprise assessments (200+ apps) may take 6-8 weeks.

What are the key deliverables?

Key deliverables include: Cloud Readiness Scorecard, Business Case Summary, Risk Assessment Report, Migration Roadmap, Executive Summary, and Detailed Assessment Report.

How do I customize the framework?

Use the industry-specific and organization-size customization guides. Adapt templates for your context, adjust scoring criteria for your priorities, and configure integration with your systems.

How do I integrate with my systems?

Follow the integration guides for CMDB, monitoring tools, cloud assessment tools, and financial systems. Each guide provides step-by-step instructions for data extraction and integration.

What support is available?

Support is available through email, Teams channel, and SharePoint feedback forms. Office hours are held weekly for questions and guidance.
