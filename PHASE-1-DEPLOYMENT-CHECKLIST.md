## Risk Mitigation

### Risk: Team Training Insufficient
- **Mitigation**: Provide detailed phase guides with step-by-step instructions
- **Mitigation**: Conduct hands-on training with sample data
- **Mitigation**: Provide quick-start guides for common scenarios
- **Mitigation**: Establish escalation procedures for questions

### Risk: Template Usability Issues
- **Mitigation**: Include detailed instructions and examples in all templates
- **Mitigation**: Conduct template walkthrough during training
- **Mitigation**: Provide sample data for hands-on practice
- **Mitigation**: Collect feedback and iterate on templates

### Risk: Calculation Accuracy Issues
- **Mitigation**: Verify all calculations with sample data before deployment
- **Mitigation**: Include calculation documentation and assumptions
- **Mitigation**: Provide error handling and validation procedures
- **Mitigation**: Establish procedures for calculation verification

### Risk: First Assessment Delays
- **Mitigation**: Confirm stakeholder availability and commitment early
- **Mitigation**: Prepare assessment team thoroughly before starting
- **Mitigation**: Establish clear timelines and milestones
- **Mitigation**: Identify and resolve blockers quickly
