## Framework Capabilities

### Assessment Scope
- **Applications**: 10 to 500+ applications
- **Infrastructure**: Comprehensive infrastructure profiling
- **Cloud Platforms**: AWS, Azure, Google Cloud evaluation
- **Industries**: Adaptable to financial services, healthcare, retail, manufacturing, etc.
- **Organization Sizes**: Small (10-50 apps), Mid-market (50-200 apps), Enterprise (200+ apps)

### Assessment Dimensions
1. **Technical Readiness**: Architecture, dependencies, technology stack, platform support
2. **Operational Readiness**: Automation, monitoring, incident response, complexity
3. **Security Readiness**: Identity management, encryption, vulnerability management, controls
4. **Compliance Readiness**: Regulatory requirements, audit trails, data residency, certifications
5. **Business Readiness**: Stakeholder alignment, budget, business case, strategic fit

### Financial Analysis
- Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) calculation
- Return on Investment (ROI) analysis
- Payback period calculation
- Net Present Value (NPV) analysis
- Sensitivity analysis for different scenarios

### Risk Management
- Security risk assessment
- Operational risk assessment
- Compliance risk assessment
- Risk mitigation strategy development
- Contingency planning

### Migration Planning
- Dependency analysis and mapping
- Migration wave grouping and sequencing
- Resource planning and allocation
- Timeline and milestone planning
- Cutover and rollback procedures

## Assessment Timeline

### Typical Assessment Duration
- **Small Organizations (10-50 apps)**: 12-16 weeks
- **Mid-Market Organizations (50-200 apps)**: 16-20 weeks
- **Enterprise Organizations (200+ apps)**: 20-24 weeks

### Phase Breakdown
| Phase | Duration | Activities |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| **Discovery** | 4-8 weeks | Stakeholder engagement, scope definition, application/infrastructure inventory, dependency mapping |
| **Analysis** | 3-6 weeks | Technical, operational, security, compliance, business assessments |
| **Evaluation** | 3-5 weeks | Multi-cloud evaluation, business case development, risk assessment, wave planning |
| **Planning** | 2-4 weeks | Detailed roadmap, resource planning, governance, change management |
